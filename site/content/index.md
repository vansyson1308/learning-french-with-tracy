# {{APP_NAME}}

A free French course for English speakers that runs entirely on your phone: a structured learning path from zero to a **CEFR-aligned A1 estimate**, with listening, reading, speaking, writing and short spoken conversations, spaced review that decides what to practise today, and no account, no ads and no analytics.

## What you get

- **A learning path** in six sections: everyday words and phrases, gender and articles, high-yield verbs, cognates and false friends, numbers, connected French (liaison and elision), listening and reading, speaking, writing, and conversations.
- **Today** — one tap starts a session that mixes review, new material and practice by memory risk, powered by a spaced-repetition scheduler. You never rate yourself; your answers are the evidence.
- **Listening** with recorded clips and **reading** passages with tap-to-look-up words; a searchable **vocabulary** browser with pronunciation and examples.
- **Speaking**: repeat-after-me practice and "say it yourself" tasks graded on what your device's speech recogniser hears; short guided **conversations** with a partner voice.
- **Writing**: guided notes and forms with instant, specific feedback.
- **Checkpoints**, a **starting-point check** so you can skip what you already know, and an **A1 capstone**.
- **Backups**: export your progress to a file and import it on another device.

## What it is not

- It is not an official or certified CEFR result. The Goals screen shows, per skill, what you have demonstrated in *this course's own checks* — an estimate, clearly labelled as such.
- It does not score your pronunciation; it checks whether your device's recogniser understood the words.
- It does not need the internet — with one exception you are told about in the app: speaking exercises use your device's system speech recogniser, which may use the internet if your device has no on-device French model.

## Price and privacy

Free. No purchases, subscriptions or advertising. No account. Progress stays on your device; backups are files you create yourself. Details: [Privacy Policy](privacy/).
