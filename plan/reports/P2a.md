Changed:
- **i18n core (`src/lib/i18n/`).** `format.ts`: a tiny ICU-style formatter — `{name}`, `{n, plural, =0 {…} one {…} other {…}}` with `#`, `{x, select, …}`, `{items, list, and|or|comma}` with per-language joiners, nesting, cached parse, malformed templates render as themselves; no `Intl` dependency (English one/other and Vietnamese other-only are hard rules, so nothing depends on Hermes ICU data — the `Intl.PluralRules` question is moot). `translate.ts` (store-free: catalogues, `translate`, `translateMessage`, `languageName`); `index.ts` (`resolveUiLanguage(pref, deviceTags)` — explicit `vi`/`en` win, `system` = Vietnamese on a `vi*` device, English otherwise, `EXPO_PUBLIC_UI_LANG` pins `system` for exports; `t()`/`tm()` usable outside React; `useT()` hook → `{t, tm, languageName, lang}`); `device-locale.ts` (`expo-localization` read once, fail-safe to English); `message.ts` (`Message = {key, params}`, `msg()` — the only i18n import engines are allowed).
