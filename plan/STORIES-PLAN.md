# Graded stories: coverage plan (coordinator QC 2026-10-28 §4)

Goal: every syllabus lexeme meets the learner in at least one context (the
P3f context rule). A context is one of:
- a lesson exercise that grades the lexeme;
- a reading or listening clip that references it;
- a graded story that uses it.

The measure is `content/reports/<lang>/context-coverage.json` (generated by
`bun run content:compile`; French: `content/reports/context-coverage.json`).
It lists every syllabus lexeme without context, unit by unit. Stories are
written against that list.

## Target per language: 40–50 stories, A1-1 → A2-1

| Band | Stories | Length | New lexemes per story |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1-1 | 10–12 | 80–150 tokens (the validator floor is 150 today; lowering it for A1-1 is a validator change with its own test) | 3–5 |
| A1-2 | 15–18 | 150–250 | 3–5 |
| A2-1 | 15–20 | 200–400 | 4–5 |

Each story is written for one unit's missing list, in unit order. It uses
the unit's lexemes in natural sentences. Its declared new lexemes come from
the same list, so a story closes about 8–15 gaps: 3–5 declared new words
plus the unit's already-taught words used in context. With 45 stories the
remaining gap is expected to close; the report confirms it.

## Order of work

1. **de (P3d(de))**: readings, listening and lessons first (they close most
   of the gap), then about 45 stories unit by unit. Audio comes from the
   audio workflow.
2. **es top-up (same pass)**: 25 more stories against the 286 missing
   lexemes; A1-1 first.
3. **fr top-up (same pass)**:
   - Derive the A1 syllabus from the French lexicon (frequency bands, like
     es/de). The current 126-word syllabus is the pack only.
   - Then add about 30 stories. French stays byte-identical except for the
     new stories and the syllabus file this task adds.
4. **it, pt (P3d(it), P3d(pt))**: 40–50 stories each, written while the
   course is authored.
5. **ja, zh, ko, en (P4c–P4f)**: the same target, with per-script token
   rules.

STATUS tracks each language's context-coverage share and story count.
