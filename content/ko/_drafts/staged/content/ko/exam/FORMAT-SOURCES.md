## TOPIK I (paper-based, PBT) — 제3조제2항, 〔별표 1〕

| Section | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 듣기 (listening) | 30 | 40 min |
| 읽기 (reading) | 40 | 60 min |
| **Total** | **70** | **100 min** |

Level by the total of the section scores: **1급 80–139**, **2급 140–200**. TOPIK I has no writing or speaking section.

## TOPIK I (internet-based, IBT) — same articles

듣기 30 items / 40 min, 읽기 30 items / 45 min (85 min). Levels: 1급 132–207, 2급 208–400.

## Not stated by the regulation (the course does not invent them)

- points per item and per section (only level ranges by total score);
- the number of answer options; the item types within each section.

## What the course does with it

- Section 3 (understanding) and the reading / listening items of the capstone are the TOPIK I-aligned practice: listening
  and reading of the kinds TOPIK I tests (signs, notices, short messages, short dialogues, announcements).
- Course choice: these items are 4-option choices.
- No TOPIK item is reproduced; every item is original.
