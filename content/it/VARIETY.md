# Italian variety — it-vi (P3d(it), QC 2026-10-29 §3.4)

**Production model: standard Italian (italiano standard).**
Every target-language text the course models uses the standard form:
- lesson prompts, options and sentences;
- readings and clip transcripts;
- speech targets and model answers;
- conversation partner lines;
- checkpoint, placement and CILS A1 items.

Reasons:
- CILS A1 is written in standard Italian.
- Standard Italian is understood everywhere in Italy, and every textbook teaches it.
- The two course voices (Kokoro `im_nicola`, `if_sara`) speak it.

Regional forms appear only as variant notes, never as a taught model:
- *cornetto* (central and southern Italy) is the course word; in the north people also say *brioche*.
- *adesso* and *ora* are both taught; *mo'* (southern) is not.
- The northern preference for the passato prossimo over the passato remoto is the course's only past tense at A1.
- In the north, *la Maria* (article before a woman's first name) is common; the course does not teach it.

Vietnamese learners going to a particular region will meet more forms; the speech grader accepts the standard
form only, plus the alternatives listed per item.
